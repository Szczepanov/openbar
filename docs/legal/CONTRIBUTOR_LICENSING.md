# Contributor licensing policy

## Current rule

OpenBar is MIT-licensed (ADR-0011). Contributions are accepted under the same licence: by
submitting a pull request, you agree that your contribution is licensed under the MIT License
(inbound = outbound). No CLA is required.

## What contributors must have the right to share

Submit only code, text, data, media or models that you wrote yourself, or that you have the right
to release under MIT. Third-party material must carry its source and a licence compatible with
MIT. Record it in the PR, and in the fixture manifest for media and data.

## Out of scope

Material that cannot be released under MIT does not belong in the repository. That includes
proprietary competitor material, non-commercial or copyleft models, and private footage. See
`docs/clean-room/COMPETITOR_BOUNDARIES.md`.
