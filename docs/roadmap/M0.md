# M0 — Validated Bar-Path Engine

## Objective

Prove the measurement chain before committing to automatic detection or app development.

## Review outcome — 2026-10-02

The M0 go/no-go review in **ADR-0008** records **NO-GO / REWORK** for M1 entry.

The architecture and deterministic headless pipeline are suitable for continued validation work, but
the evidence does not yet justify moving up-stack:

- plate-centre accuracy and availability are not measured on held-out annotated real footage;
- no production tracker/filter is selected;
- no real-world recording condition is yet classified fully supported;
- ROM/mean/peak velocity accuracy lacks an independent physical reference;
- phone-class offline performance is unmeasured.

M1 remains locked pending the re-entry work in #57, #53, #58 and #59 and a new explicit GO or
CONDITIONAL GO review.

Completing/closing the M0 umbrella after ADR-0008 is merged means the milestone reached an
evidence-based decision. It does **not** mean the measurement hypothesis passed.

## Work packages


### 1. Dataset and fixtures

- Collect 20–30 redistribution-safe side-view clips across several lifts, frame rates, lighting conditions, plate colours, distances, and controlled tracking challenges.
- For 5–10 representative clips, annotate plate centre positions at a useful interval.
- Record provenance and redistribution rights for every fixture.
- Keep private/non-redistributable research material outside the public repository.

### 2. Manual initialization

Define a simple plate-target seed: centre plus radius/bounding box at a selected timestamp.

No automatic detector is required for M0.

### 3. Tracking spike

Evaluate at least two plausible approaches under the same fixtures. Candidate families include feature/optical-flow tracking, correlation/template approaches, and detector-assisted tracking.

Track loss must be explicit.

The first M0 experiment provides two decoder-agnostic Rust baselines behind one contract:
fixed-template matching and local-contrast centroid tracking. Their procedural comparison is a
regression/contract gate only; both remain candidates until real decoded-video fixtures provide
sufficient evidence for a production selection. See `docs/validation/TRACKER_EXPERIMENTS.md`.

### 4. Calibration

Start with known plate diameter calibration. Preserve calibration metadata and later document limitations from perspective/parallax/camera angle.

### 5. Filtering and kinematics

Evaluate filtering choices rather than selecting by appearance.

Initial candidates:

- raw identity baseline;
- centred moving average baseline;
- timestamp-aware Savitzky–Golay;
- constant-velocity Kalman/state-space baseline.

All four now run through a common Rust filter contract with implementation/version identity,
effective parameters, timestamp-preserving output, explicit irregular-timestamp semantics, explicit
gap/reset behaviour, documented confidence propagation, and edge/latency behaviour.
`openbar-cli filter-experiment` tunes parameters only on deterministic seeded Gaussian-noise
development signals averaged across multiple seeds and evaluates the frozen per-family
configurations on held-out synthetic signals. Peak metrics are emitted only where the reference
scenario intentionally defines a peak.

No production default is selected from synthetic evidence. Real fixture/reference evidence remains
required before promotion.

Do not differentiate noisy raw data and then present the result as authoritative.

### 6. CLI

The headless M0 integration surface is now implemented in `apps/openbar-cli`.

```text
openbar-cli analyze ...   -> canonical analysis-v1 JSON
openbar-cli benchmark ... -> versioned benchmark artifact + stderr summary
openbar-cli render ...    -> deterministic diagnostic SVG from canonical analysis
```

`analyze` requires explicit tracker, filter, plate-diameter and kinematics quality settings; it
does not silently choose a production tracker/filter or substitute a fallback. `render` consumes
that canonical output without rerunning measurements and keeps lost/low-confidence states visible.
See `docs/validation/CLI_PIPELINE.md` and `docs/validation/DIAGNOSTIC_RENDERING.md`.

### 7. Validation

Compare centre tracking and derived metrics with manually labelled/reference measurements.

### 8. Go/no-go review

ADR-0008 records **NO-GO / REWORK**. Do not proceed to automatic detection or Flutter until the
documented M1 re-entry criteria are satisfied and a subsequent review records GO or CONDITIONAL GO.

## Explicitly out of scope

- accounts/backend/cloud;
- AI coaching;
- pose estimation;
- automatic exercise recognition;
- live camera mode;
- BLE sensors;
- Apple Watch/Wear OS;
- coach/team dashboards;
- subscriptions/paywalls.


## Non-blocking research follow-ups from public VBT/CV references

The review in
[`docs/analysis/VBT_OPEN_SOURCE_REFERENCE_REVIEW.md`](../analysis/VBT_OPEN_SOURCE_REFERENCE_REVIEW.md)
records several experiments that may improve the rework evidence package without changing the M0
scope fence:

- controlled marker/fiducial tracking as a secondary benchmark reference;
- intrinsic/lens-distortion sensitivity inside the intended recording envelope;
- single-reference versus robust multi-frame plate-diameter calibration;
- a timestamp-assumption-explicit zero-phase Butterworth filter family;
- geometry-transform invariants for future model preprocessing.

These are **research candidates, not new M0 acceptance criteria**. Do not place them on the critical
path merely because another project uses them. Promote an experiment only when it answers a current
M0 re-entry uncertainty and can be evaluated under OpenBar's existing reproducibility, provenance,
licensing and held-out-evidence rules.

Execution order, prerequisites, decision gates and stop conditions are defined in
[`docs/plans/VBT_REFERENCE_EXPERIMENTS_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md`](../plans/VBT_REFERENCE_EXPERIMENTS_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md).
